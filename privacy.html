Your Information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Softouch Barbershop respects your privacy. This page explains what personal information we handle when you use this website or contact us, and why. We handle it in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who we are

Softouch Barbershop, Shop 4, 29 Nelson Mandela Drive, Mthatha (Umtata), South Africa.
Phone and WhatsApp: 067 056 7332

What we collect, and why

Booking requests. The booking form asks for your name, phone number, the service you want and your preferred date. The form does not send this information to our website or store it. It only writes a WhatsApp message on your own device. The message reaches us only if you press Send in WhatsApp. We use those details to arrange and confirm your appointment, and to contact you about it.

Calls and messages. If you phone, WhatsApp or message us on social media, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name and number. We use them to answer you and to manage your booking.

Children. If you book a haircut for a child, please make the booking yourself as their parent or guardian.

Photos

Our gallery shows haircuts we have done. We only show photos of clients who have agreed to it. If you appear in a photo and want it removed, contact us and we will take it down.

Technical information

This website does not use cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Like any website, our hosting provider may log technical details such as visitors' IP addresses. The website also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your information. Messages you send through WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp, and the privacy terms of WhatsApp and its owner apply to them. We only share your information with others if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep booking details only for as long as we need them to manage your appointment.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you may object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
